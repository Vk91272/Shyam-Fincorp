GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Important information to help you make a considered decision.

Does the EMI calculator mean my loan is approved? +

No. It provides an estimate only. Eligibility and approval depend on assessment and the lender's final decision.

What do I need to track my application? +

Use the application ID provided to you and the mobile number registered with that application.

Are there any fees or charges? +

Confirm applicable interest, fees, charges, terms and conditions in official loan documents before accepting an offer.

How should I protect my information? +

Submit personal information only through official channels. Never share passwords, OTPs or full card details. Review the official privacy policy before applying.
