GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Make an informed decision. Always read the applicable Key Fact Statement, loan agreement and repayment schedule before accepting a loan.

Does submitting an application guarantee approval?＋

No. Submission only starts the process. Any approval is subject to eligibility checks, verification, underwriting and the lender's policy.

What interest rate and fees will apply?＋

The applicable rate, APR, processing fee, taxes, late-payment charges and other terms must be communicated in the official offer and relevant documents. Do not rely on the calculator as a quote.

Can I apply without all documents ready?＋

You can start by providing the information requested in the form. Additional documents may be required before a decision; submit them only through an official secure channel.

How can I protect myself from fraud?＋

Verify that you are communicating with an authorised representative. Never share OTPs, passwords or card PINs, and do not pay an unverified person promising guaranteed approval.

What if I have a complaint or need help?＋

Use the official contact and grievance details supplied in your loan documents or on the company's verified channels. Add the organisation's verified contact details here before launch.
