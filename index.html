COMMON QUESTIONS

Things you may
want to know.

Still need help? Reach out through Shyam Fincorp’s verified official contact details.

Does submitting the form mean my loan is approved?+

No. It is an initial enquiry/application. Approval depends on verification, eligibility assessment, required checks and the lender's final decision.

What happens after I apply?+

Your details are submitted to the connected application service. If further information or documents are needed, the team will communicate the next steps.

When will disbursement show on my account?+

Disbursement should show only after required checks, approval, agreement steps and confirmation of the actual transfer. An application alone does not mean funds have been sent.

How can I check my application status?+

Use the tracking form with your application ID and registered mobile number. The result depends on the tracking API being available and your record being found.

How should I share sensitive documents?+

Use only the official secure upload process provided by the company. Never share OTPs, PINs, passwords or full banking credentials in a public form or chat.
